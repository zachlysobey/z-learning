# Climbing Volumes Resources

Sources checked 2026-09-01. Numbers quoted in lessons should trace back to one of these.

## Knowledge

- [Andy Librande, "Build a Triangle Wood Volume for Your Climbing Wall" (2012)](https://andylibrande.com/homeclimbingwall/2012/03/build-a-triangle-wood-volume-for-your-climbing-wall/)
  Home-wall builder since 2005. Full triangle build: 3/4" 7- or 9-ply, three faces (30/60/90,
  a slightly smaller nesting 30/60/90, and a 45/45/90 cap), 1.75" assembly screws, pilot holes,
  ~30 T-nuts kept 2" from the base edge, primer + latex + optional poly, mounted with 8+ screws
  (3 per side minimum). Use for: the canonical first build; realistic numbers for a home volume.
- [Xcult Climbing, volumes technical info](https://www.xcultclimbing.com/en/volumes_technical_info)
  Commercial maker's spec sheet: 18 mm birch ply, quartz sand + PU paint texture, M10 zinc-plated
  8.8 T-nuts, screw-only mounting with 4.5-5 mm screws with smooth (unribbed) heads, don't over-tighten,
  keep ply volumes away from water. Use for: what "gym quality" concretely means.
- [Commercial 3D Files (formerly Sender Grips), DIY volume plans](https://commercial3dfiles.com/collections/sender-grips-diy-climbing-volume-plans-for-gyms-and-home-walls)
  Paid plans ($6-14) for 18 mm ply: edge lengths, cut angles and miter angles per board, some with
  STEP files. Use for: seeing how a professional cut list is laid out; a cheap shortcut when a shape's
  geometry is beyond what we want to derive by hand.
- [ProVolumes, volume plan catalog](https://www.provolumes.com/volume-catalog)
  Paid plans ($10-25, bundles more): pyramids, ledges, prisms, barrels, kites. Use for: shape vocabulary
  and inspiration; the catalog page alone shows what shapes exist and roughly how they nest.
- [indoorclimbing.com, "Climbing Wall Texturing"](https://www.indoorclimbing.com/bouldering_finishing.html)
  Texture and paint recipes: ~10:1 paint to sand by volume, remix every roller dip, prime first,
  exterior latex topcoat, plug T-nuts with golf tees, galvanized fasteners because latex is water-based.
  Use for: the finishing lesson and finishing the Marketplace triangles.
- [Blocklayer pyramid calculator (inch)](https://www.blocklayer.com/pyramid-calculatoreng)
  Gives face bevel angles and dimensions for pyramids of any base and height. Use for: computing
  bevel angles for pyramid volumes before we derive them by hand; checking hand calculations.
- [EN 12572-3:2017, Artificial climbing structures, Part 3: climbing holds (paid standard)](https://webstore.ansi.org/standards/ds/dsen125722017-1647612)
  The European safety standard covering holds and volumes (size classes, fixation and breaking
  forces). Not freely readable; only relevant if the side-hustle path gets serious. Use for: knowing
  the standard exists and what it would take to claim compliance.

### Video (for seeing the cuts happen)

- [Giles Barton-Owen, "Making Volumes for climbing - Thoughts and tips" (2020)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BRPr6o8mNYI)
  Reflective home-wall builder's lessons learned after many plywood volumes. Use for: pitfalls
  before the first cut.
- [Climber Dad, "5 STEPS to BUILDING a VOLUME" (2024)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=djD9WGnyRKs),
  ["The Penta-volume" (2018)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=58qfINTyizQ),
  ["How to Paint a Volume" ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JcTZ_VxQIZc)
  Home-wall channel with several volume builds and a dedicated painting/texture video.
  Use for: watching a full build end to end; the paint video for finishing.
- [Aux'Arc Climbing, "Building Volumes out of Scrap Plywood" (2020)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DShfb5t9-KM)
  Small screw-on volumes from offcuts. Use for: the "use what you have" path, relevant to the gym panels.
- [Felix Jockenhövel, "How to build a Pyramid Climbing Volume (EASY DIY)" (2023)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xwamzS548KY)
  Pyramid build with ply and screws. Use for: a second shape after the triangle.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/homewall](https://www.reddit.com/r/homewall/)
  The home climbing wall subreddit; volume builds and "does this look safe" posts are common.
  Use for: posting build photos for critique, asking about the gym-panel conversion idea.
- [Mountain Project forums](https://www.mountainproject.com/forum)
  Long-running climbing forum with a training/home-wall section. Use for: searching old threads on
  volume construction and mounting.

## Gaps

- No verified source yet on deriving compound bevel angles for non-pyramid shapes (ledges, kites,
  asymmetric triangles) by hand; the calculators and paid plans cover the "what", not the "why".
  Search for a woodworking (not climbing) source on compound miters when that lesson comes up.
- No source yet on cutting sheet goods accurately without a table saw (circular saw + straightedge,
  track saw). Needed before any tool-buying decision.
- No source found on reusing textured, T-nutted gym panels as volume stock. Likely a community question.
