# Starting point: no saw, very little woodworking, but real materials on hand

Zach has done very little woodworking; bevels and miters are new. He owns a drill and impact driver,
basic hand tools, some T-nuts and hardware, wall panels from defunct climbing gyms, and two rough,
unfinished Marketplace triangle volumes. Workspace is an unfinished basement. He wants fundamentals,
not a rush to output, and may have little hands-on time for a while.

**Implications**: start from anatomy and inspection of the objects he already owns rather than a
build. Any tool purchase (circular saw + guide, track saw) is itself a lesson, justified by a
specific cut. The gym panels are a design constraint worth a dedicated lesson: pre-textured,
pre-T-nutted faces change what shapes are practical. See [[MISSION.md]].
