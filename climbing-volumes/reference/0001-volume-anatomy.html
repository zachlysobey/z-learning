Inspection checklist

For any volume you pick up, second-hand or your own:

  1. Count faces; sketch the base outline.
  2. Measure ply thickness at an exposed edge.
  3. Miter or butt joint? Gaps?
  4. Screws per seam; glue?
  5. Base flat on the floor, or rocking?
  6. T-nuts: present, size, distance from edges.

Vocabulary

Bevel
An edge cut at an angle other than 90° through the thickness of the board.
Miter joint
Two bevelled edges meeting so no end grain shows.
Butt joint
A square-cut edge fastened against the face of another board.
Screw-on / bolt-on
How a volume (or hold) attaches: screws into the wall ply, or a bolt into a T-nut.
Nominal thickness
The named size (¾"); actual is thinner (23/32").
